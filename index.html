FOOD FACT

Fruit or Vegetable?

Botanically, tomato is a fruit because it develops from the flower of the plant and contains seeds.

In everyday cooking, however, we commonly use it like a vegetable.

NUTRITION

What's Inside a Tomato?

Tomatoes are mostly water and provide a range of nutrients and naturally occurring plant compounds.

💧 Water Tomatoes have a high water content.
🍊 Vitamin C Contributes to normal immune system function.
🌿 Potassium A mineral naturally present in tomatoes.
🔴 Lycopene A naturally occurring plant compound that gives many tomatoes their red colour.
NIJOK NOTE

Nutrition can vary with tomato variety, size, ripeness and preparation. Facts help you understand your food — you decide what is right for you.
