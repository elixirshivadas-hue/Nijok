FOOD FACT

Fruit or Vegetable?

Botanically, tomato is a fruit because it develops from the flower of the plant and contains seeds.

In everyday cooking, however, we commonly use it like a vegetable.

🍅 A Natural Source of Lycopene

Tomatoes contain lycopene, a naturally occurring compound responsible for much of their red colour.

The amount can vary depending on the tomato variety, ripeness and how it is prepared.
